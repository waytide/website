# The Site's Content Areas — Aspiration

## Summary

A **running list of the content areas the site carries** — the bodies of material it has to
cover, added to as areas surface. It is a list rather than an argument: each entry names an area,
says what it establishes, and records what it rests on.

**A content area is not a point the website should make.** The two are easy to blur and are kept
apart deliberately:

- A **point** is a claim to make, and it belongs to persuasion — *the cycle is chosen, not
  performed*. Points live in *Producing and Presenting Waytide's Self-Explanation — Design*.
- A **content area** is a body of material the site has to carry, and it belongs to coverage —
  what a reader can come here and learn. An area may contain several points, or none.

**This is the beginning of an answer to what fills the site**, which that design records as its
nearest-term open question. It does not close it: the areas accumulate here, and how they are
produced from the projection is still unsettled.

## Content areas

### An agent that is an ally

**A description of an agent that is an ally**, and what makes it one.

**It establishes "ally" as a kind of configuration of an agent** — not a disposition, a
personality, or a claim about intent. It is how the agent is set up. That framing is what makes
the word checkable rather than promotional: a configuration can be inspected, and either the
arrangements are in place or they are not.

**What the configuration predisposes the agent toward is working with the human along the
human's goal vector and process, rather than displacing it.** Displacement is the failure the
area is written against, and it is worth naming concretely, because it rarely looks like
disobedience — it looks like helpfulness. An agent displaces the human when it substitutes its
own reading of the goal for theirs, proceeds where the decision was the human's to make, or
optimizes toward its own notion of done. The ally configuration is the set of arrangements that
make that harder to do than to avoid.

**Unattended work is explicitly opt-in, and must be so affirmed to the agent before it proceeds
unattended.** This is the sharpest instance of the whole area, and the one that can be checked
against the system rather than taken on assurance:

- **Attended is the default**, and the choice of cadence is put to the developer at **every**
  initiation of a feature or an experiment, through the selection interface.
- **Silence does not authorize unattended work.** The agent may not infer it from the size of the
  task, from a developer's absence, or from a run having gone smoothly so far. It proceeds
  unattended only on an affirmative choice.
- **The mode is recorded** — as a confirmation in the feature or experiment record, and declared
  at the top of the loop record, because it changes how every cycle below is read.
- **DBE remains in force either way.** Unattended is not the method switched off; it is the
  gating switched off. The cradle, the efferent-first actuation, and the solubility standard all
  still hold.

**What it rests on.** The `design-by-efferent` package's
`attended-or-unattended-is-chosen-at-initiation` rule, and foundation's `feature-lifecycle` and
`experiment-lifecycle` rules, which put the choice at initiation. The area describes what those
rules amount to; it does not assert anything beyond them.

**Why it belongs on the site.** *Ally* is the word most at risk of reading as marketing, and it
is load-bearing — it forecloses the reading that a system of rules, records, and gates is process
compliance imposed on the human. A reader has no reason to accept the word on assurance. Showing
it as a configuration, with opt-in unattended work as the instance, replaces a claim about
disposition with something a reader can go and check.

## Settled

- **2026-08-03** — **The site's content areas are kept as a running list**, here, in the design
  directory. An area is a body of material the site carries, distinct from a point the website
  should make.
- **2026-08-03** — **The first content area is a description of an agent that is an ally**,
  establishing *ally* as a **configuration** of an agent that predisposes it to work with the
  human along the human's goal vector and process rather than displacing it, with **unattended
  work as explicitly opt-in** and requiring affirmation before the agent proceeds unattended.

## Out of Scope / Deferred

- **How an area becomes pages.** An area is not a page, a section, or a navigation entry, and
  nothing here says how one is turned into any of them.
- **Which areas the projection can produce, and which need writing.** The site's content is meant
  to be derived rather than authored. An area resting on rules — as the first one does — is
  derivable; an area resting on reasoning that lives only in designs and session records may not
  be.
- **The order the areas are covered in**, and which are needed before the site is worth
  publishing.
- **Whether an area maps onto the documentation, the blog, or the showcase** of the near-term
  site's shape.
